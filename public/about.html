About NioPets

A small shop with a short list, run by people who share their homes with animals.

Our story

Six products beats six thousand

Most pet shops list everything and recommend nothing. We went the other way. We buy the top contenders in a category, live with them for a month, and list the one that survives.

That is why our catalogue is short. If something better comes along we swap it out. If nothing does, the list stays as it is.

  • ✓ Products tested at home, not chosen from a supplier catalogue
  • ✓ Honest shipping estimates, we would rather under promise
  • ✓ Support answered by people who own the products
